Started in Helsinki in 2014.

East Studios was started in 2014 by Oliver Kulpakko in Helsinki, Finland, as a home for his app projects. Since then we've made more than 20 different apps, and six of them are available today.

We'd rather keep a few apps well maintained than ship many and leave them behind. The apps that are still out there get updates, support and real answers from the people who built them.
